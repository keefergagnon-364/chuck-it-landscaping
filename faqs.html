Frequently asked

What customers want to know.

How much will my yard project cost?

Cost depends on the property, the amount of material or debris, access, labor, and the service requested. We offer free on-site estimates so you can receive a quote based on the actual job.

How quickly can the work be done?

Timing depends on the size of the job, weather, materials, and the current route. We will review the project and discuss a realistic schedule with you. We aim to respond to new inquiries within 24 hours.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Chuck-it offers free on-site estimates for work within the service area.

What areas does Chuck-it serve?

Chuck-it works throughout Arizona's White Mountains, including Show Low, Pinetop, Lakeside, Linden, Snowflake, and Heber. Ask if your property is just outside those areas.

Can I schedule recurring yard care?

Yes. Recurring mowing, cleanup, and property-care schedules may be available depending on the location and route. Include your preferred frequency when requesting an estimate.

Does Chuck-it offer snow removal?

Seasonal snow removal is planned and availability can vary with weather and route capacity. Contact us to ask about current service.

Do you install pavers or irrigation?

No. Chuck-it does not currently offer paver installation or irrigation services.

What happens after I request a quote?

We will review your information and follow up by phone, text, or email—usually within 24 hours—to learn more and arrange an on-site estimate when needed.
